# YouTube title

AI vs Machine Learning vs Deep Learning vs Generative AI — Animated

## Description

Artificial intelligence, machine learning, deep learning, and generative AI are four different sizes of the same idea. This film puts them in four balls and leaves the gaps visible.

A chess program is already AI, and it never learns. Machine learning is the ball inside that, where a score comes from examples. Deep learning is the ball inside that, where a dog picture passes through many layers. Generative AI is not the innermost kind of intelligence. It is the job of making something new. Most of it sits inside deep learning. A cap of it does not.

A large language model continues text. A chatbot is the application in front of the model.

About seven minutes. No training lecture, no tokens, no safety catalogue.

References, same map, different pictures:

- [IBM Technology: AI, Machine Learning, Deep Learning and Generative AI Explained](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qYNweeDHiyU)
- [Krish Naik: AI vs ML vs DL vs Generative AI](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=X7Zd4VyUgL0)

## What this film is

One object, four solid balls. The spoken track is the **Say** lines. With the holds, the film is about seven minutes. The value is the picture: the same terms Jeff Crume and Krish Naik place in a lecture, shown as spheres that grow, stop short, and then refuse the usual centre.

The desktop diagram `ai-venn.png` is the belief we build and then correct. Generative AI is not a fourth nested subset.

## What this film does not open

Training and inference as a lesson, parameters, data science, supervised and the other learning types, tokens, attention, sampling, foundation-model taxonomy, hallucinations, bias, privacy, or deepfakes. The long email script still owns those. A later film can zoom into one ball.

## Picture rules

- The four balls are the only diagram. Nothing becomes a new chapter card.
- Show the behaviour, then the label.
- The chessboard, once placed in the outer gap, never moves into an inner ball.
- The popular center diagram appears once, holds, and is the same object that then moves. Do not cut to a second chart.
- The host is on camera only for the opening.

Read the **Say** lines aloud. The **Show** lines are the picture at that moment.

## 1. Opening

**Say**

AI is not what you think.

People talk as if there is no AI until a model learns. That is not true. AI was here before machine learning. It still exists outside it.

**Show**

Selfie, close, already looking into the lens. On the last sentence, cut to black `#070A16`. No title card. The face does not return.

## 2. The outer ball

**Say**

Watch.

**Show**

A solid cyan ball grows from the centre until it owns the frame, fill `#17647D`. Leave room for the legend and the Kavriq bubble. Do not label it yet.

A small chessboard fades up inside the ball, clearly inside, not pasted on the rim. Pieces are simple discs. White plays a short legal sequence. Each move is chosen by a visible stack of three rules beside the board:

- **CHECK THE POSITION**
- **FOLLOW THE RULES**
- **PICK A MOVE**

No dataset. No score. No "learning" word. When the sequence ends, the board sits still.

**Say**

This is already AI.

AI is the broad goal: a computer doing something we associate with intelligence. Learning is optional.

A classical chess program does this with search a person designed. It does not study old games. Leave the rules alone, and tomorrow it plays the same way. Programs like this were called expert systems. They were AI long before a model learned anything.

**Show**

On "This is already AI," stamp the label on the upper interior of the cyan ball: **ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE**. Light **AI** in the legend.

On the expert-system line, a small plate sits next to the rule stack: **WRITTEN RULES · NO LEARNING**. The chessboard stays lit. Do not add a second example.

## 3. The ball that stops short

**Say**

Now a second ball.

**Show**

A green ball, `#1E7A55`, grows from the same centre. For a moment it looks as if it will fill the cyan ball. It stops early. The background gutter between green and cyan is obvious. The chessboard is pushed outward and comes to rest in that gap, still on cyan, still upright, rules still readable.

Hold. Do not speak over the first half-second. The gap is the reveal.

Inside the green ball, five small cards slide in one at a time. Three are marked **SPAM**. Two are marked **NOT SPAM**. They are examples, not a lesson in training. The cards compress into the ball. A new, unmarked card enters. A single result prints on it: **SPAM · 96%**.

**Say**

The green ball learned a pattern from examples and used it on something new. That is machine learning. It sits inside AI. It does not fill AI.

The chess program is still out here. It never saw those examples.

**Show**

On "That is machine learning," label the green ball **MACHINE LEARNING**. Light **ML**.

A thin arrow inside the green ball reads **EXAMPLES → SCORE**. Leave the chessboard and its plate in the gap. Pulse the gap once, not the whole diagram.

## 4. Many layers

**Say**

Inside machine learning, one more ball.

**Show**

A violet ball, `#53368A`, grows from the centre and stops inside the green ball, with its own gutter. The chessboard does not move. The spam card stays in the green ring, outside the violet ball.

Inside violet, a dog photograph enters from the left and becomes a short grid of numbers. The grid passes through three visible layers of dots, left to right. The first layer flashes simple edges. The second flashes a few shapes. The third resolves a result beside the layers: **DOG**.

Do not build a large network, a weight lecture, or a training loop. A small caption under the layers, with the result: **LAYERS ALREADY SET**. This pass only answers.

**Say**

Deep learning is machine learning that uses a neural network with many layers. It is called deep for one reason. Many layers sit between the input and the answer.

You will hear that this copies the brain. It does not. Each unit takes numbers in and sends a number on. That number can be zero. The name is a metaphor. The ball is mathematics.

**Show**

On "Deep learning is," label the violet ball **DEEP LEARNING**. Light **DL**.

On "It does not," a faint brain outline tries to settle on the violet ball and fails. It cracks into the same dots already there. No biology remains on screen.

## 5. The diagram people already believe

**Say**

You have seen the next picture.

**Show**

Match `ai-venn.png` for one held beat. A fourth ball, in that stock green, drops into the dead centre of the violet ball and sits fully inside it. Label it, in that stock style: **GENERATIVE AI**.

The other three labels remain. For this beat only, the nesting is the familiar onion: AI contains machine learning, which contains deep learning, which contains generative AI. Hold it still long enough to recognise. Do not correct it yet.

**Say**

It looks finished. Most explanations stop here. Generative AI is drawn as the innermost kind of intelligence.

It isn't.

## 6. The correction

**Say**

Generative AI is a job, not a smaller mind. It makes something that was not in the input.

**Show**

The centre ball is the same object. Recolour it to rose `#C4485C` as it starts to move. It swells. Most of its body stays inside the violet ball. Where rose covers violet, use the solid overlap `#35194F`, not transparency. A cap of the rose ball crosses the violet gutter and comes to rest outside deep learning, still inside the cyan ball. Part of that cap also crosses the green gutter, so a little rose sits on pure cyan. That sliver is required.

While the ball moves, three products appear inside the rose body and shrink to icons:

- A short line of text writing itself: **Thank you for coming.**
- A simple generated face, clearly synthetic, no celebrity.
- A one-second voice waveform.

They are examples of the job. They do not get their own lecture.

**Say**

A rule-based program can complete a sentence from a template. A formula can draw a curve. Those systems generate, and they are not deep learning. Some of them are not machine learning either. That is the sliver on the outer cyan.

Most systems people mean today do use these layers. That is why most of the rose ball stays inside the violet one. The job is older than the network. The network is not the definition.

A large language model is one modern way to do the text part. It continues text. A chatbot is the application in front of that model. The window is not a new ball.

**Show**

On "It makes something that was not in the input," replace the stock centre label with **GENERATIVE AI · MAKES SOMETHING NEW**. Light **GenAI**.

On the template line, a tiny card flickers in the rose sliver on pure cyan: **TEMPLATE · NO LEARNING**. It is kin to the chess plate. It fades to a small mark so the sliver stays readable.

On the large-language-model sentence, the text icon gains a chip: **CONTINUES TEXT**. On "chatbot," a phone window frames that chip and is labelled **APP**. The window shrinks off. The chip stays on the ball. Do not add a fifth sphere. Do not say "token."

Leave the spam card and the dog result in place. A small caption on the green and violet results: **CHOOSES A LABEL**. The rose ball keeps **MAKES SOMETHING NEW**.

## 7. The gap

**Say**

Look at the gap we never closed.

**Show**

The camera eases out until all four balls and the gutters are equally readable. The chessboard is still in the cyan gap. Pulse only that gap.

**Say**

The chess program is still out here.

It is AI. It is not machine learning. It is not deep learning. It never generated a new sentence from examples.

Four words. Four different sizes. Only the outer ball is the whole field.

**Show**

End hold, all still visible:

- Cyan, **ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE**, chessboard in the gap, plate **WRITTEN RULES · NO LEARNING**
- Green, **MACHINE LEARNING**, **SPAM · 96%**, caption **CHOOSES A LABEL**
- Violet, **DEEP LEARNING**, **DOG**, three layers
- Rose, mostly inside violet, cap outside it, **GENERATIVE AI · MAKES SOMETHING NEW**, chip **CONTINUES TEXT**

No subscribe line over the hold. The legend is already complete. A next film can open the green ball, or the rose one. The face does not come back. The gap is the summary.
