## Left side: complexity

Stack the primary terms:

> **AI**  
> **MACHINE LEARNING**  
> **DEEP LEARNING**  
> **GENERATIVE AI**

Place a simplified neural-network diagram beneath or behind the words.

I would not add **Neural Network** as another equally large line. The diagram already communicates it. If we want the phrase, place it in smaller violet text beneath the network.

Use the stable colors:

- AI — cyan
- Machine Learning — green
- Deep Learning / network — violet
- Generative AI — pink

The words should feel dense but organized—not like a random word cloud.

## Center: thunderbolt

Use a thick vertical thunderbolt that divides the two worlds.

Put **VS** in the center of it.

The thunderbolt could transition from cyan/violet on its left edge to orange/pink on its right edge. Keep **VS** white and large.

One caveat: this does not literally mean that the terminology opposes the email. It means “complex theory versus practical explanation.” The composition should make that interpretation obvious.

## Right side: simplicity

Use:

> **ONE EMAIL**

Make this larger than every individual term on the left.

Below it, show one large envelope. The envelope can be partially open, with the email card emerging from it.

Underneath, use three compact chips:

> **SPAM 96%** · **DOG 98%** · **AI DRAFT**

These are stronger than:

> Spam · Attachment · Auto Response

because they show actual results and create more curiosity. “Attachment” is an input, while “Dog: 98%” shows what the AI discovered. “AI Draft” is also clearer than “Auto Response,” because the system drafts rather than automatically sending the reply.

Chip colors:

- **SPAM 96%** — green
- **DOG 98%** — violet
- **AI DRAFT** — pink

A very small coral **TUE → WED** warning could sit on the draft, but only if it remains legible. It should not become another headline.

## Visual contrast

Make the two sides intentionally different:

- Left: dense, technical, interconnected
- Right: spacious, concrete, immediately understandable
- Left background: subtle network grid
- Right background: clean dark panel
- Center bolt: bright and energetic

The final text hierarchy would be:

1. **ONE EMAIL**
2. **VS**
3. AI / Machine Learning / Deep Learning / Generative AI
4. SPAM 96% · DOG 98% · AI DRAFT

This is stronger than the previous “4 Systems, 1 Mistake” concept for this particular title because it directly sells the video’s promise: turning a crowded collection of terms into one practical example.
